Fernlet support

Write to fernletapp@gmail.com — a real person reads every message.

Helpful things to include

  • What you were doing, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • A screenshot, if the issue is visible.

A note on your data

Fernlet has no accounts and we hold none of your data, so there is no data we can look up, recover, or delete for you — your information lives on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. Export, deletion, and backup controls are in the app under Settings → Privacy & Data. Details in the privacy policy.

If you're in crisis

Fernlet is a self-care companion, not a medical service. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services — or, in the US and Canada, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
