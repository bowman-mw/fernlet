✿ Fernlet

A gentle self-care companion — a tamagotchi of yourself.

Fernlet is an iPhone app where a little companion creature reflects how you're caring for yourself — food, movement, sleep, journaling, rest. No streaks to lose, no optimization pressure. Gentle, consistent care.

Privacy first, by construction

Fernlet has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no servers of ours. What you log lives on your phone; optional backup goes to your own iCloud, encrypted so not even Apple can read the sensitive parts. Friend features work only in person, phone-to-phone. The full story is in the privacy policy.
