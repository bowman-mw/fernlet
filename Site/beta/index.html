New to TestFlight?

TestFlight is Apple's own app for trying apps before they reach the App Store. It's free, and the beta of Fernlet lives inside it until the real one is out.

Getting set up

1

Install TestFlight

Search for TestFlight in the App Store on your iPhone. It's made by Apple and costs nothing.

2

Open the invite on your iPhone

The email from TestFlight has a View in TestFlight button. Tap it on your iPhone and TestFlight opens to Fernlet. If it asks for a code instead, the code is in the same email.

3

Tap Install

Fernlet appears on your Home Screen like any other app. A small orange dot beside its name marks it as a beta.

While you're testing

Sending feedback

Take a screenshot, tap it, then tap Share Beta Feedback. You can draw on it and add a note. Or open TestFlight, choose Fernlet, and tap Send Beta Feedback. If Fernlet crashes, TestFlight offers to send a note about what you were doing.

New builds

TestFlight lets you know when a new build is ready. Turn on Automatic Updates on Fernlet's page in TestFlight and it updates by itself.

Builds expire

Each beta build works for 90 days. There'll be a newer one long before then; just keep updating.

Leaving the beta

Any time you like: open TestFlight, choose Fernlet, and tap Stop Testing. No hard feelings.

What you'll need

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later

Fernlet is iPhone only for now.

iPhone 15 Pro or newer, for the AI parts

The on-device AI needs Apple Intelligence. Everything else works without it, with simpler wording in its place.

A friend in the beta, for Friends

Friends begin in person, so trying them takes two phones in the same room.

A little patience

It's a beta. Expect rough edges, and builds that change from week to week.

What I'll see

Your email is used for the beta and nothing else: no mailing list, nothing passed on. Apple sends the TestFlight invite, so the address goes into App Store Connect to do that.

From TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's service, not Fernlet's. It shows me the name and email you joined with, which build you have installed, how often each build is opened or crashes, and any screenshots and comments you send.

From the app: nothing

The beta is the same app with the same promises. It has no analytics and no account, and your meals, map, journal and cycle stay on your iPhone, exactly as the privacy policy says.
