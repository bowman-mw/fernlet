Closed beta

Help shape Fernlet before it ships.

Fernlet is almost ready for the App Store. First, a small group of people will use it day to day through TestFlight, Apple's app for trying apps before they're released, and tell me what feels right and what doesn't.

Email to join the beta or write to fernletapp@gmail.com with “Beta” in the subject
1

Send one email

The button opens an email that's already filled in. Add your name, your iPhone model and your iOS version (Settings → General → About shows both), and send it from the address you'd like the invite to go to.

2

Get a TestFlight invite

When there's a place for you, Apple emails you an invite. Install TestFlight from the App Store, open the invite on your iPhone, and Fernlet installs like any other app. New builds arrive through TestFlight.

3

Use it, and tell me

Use Fernlet the way you'd use it anyway. When something's confusing, broken or lovely, take a screenshot and send it from TestFlight, or just reply to the email. There are no surveys and no minimum.

What you'll need

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later

Fernlet is iPhone only for now.

iPhone 15 Pro or newer, for the AI parts

The on-device AI needs Apple Intelligence. Everything else works without it, with simpler wording in its place.

A friend in the beta, for Friends

Friends begin in person, so trying them takes two phones in the same room.

A little patience

It's a beta. Expect rough edges, and builds that change from week to week.

What I'll see

Your email is used for the beta and nothing else: no mailing list, nothing passed on. Apple sends the TestFlight invite, so the address goes into App Store Connect to do that.

From TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's service, not Fernlet's. It shows me the name and email you joined with, which build you have installed, how often each build is opened or crashes, and any screenshots and comments you send.

From the app: nothing

The beta is the same app with the same promises. It has no analytics and no account, and your meals, map, journal and cycle stay on your iPhone, exactly as the privacy policy says.

What's in it, and what's next

Built by one person, in the open. Most of this list has turned into real features already — what's left keeps the same rule: nothing here changes the privacy commitments.

  • Tapping phones to meet, hangouts and friend shops

  • The Disposable camera, the photo strip and Album
  • Creation Studio clothing sharing

  • Guided workouts with weights from your own history, animated how-tos and a Bodybuilding goal
  • Sealed backups and trainer session exports
  • Spanish, throughout the app
Being finished now

Coming next
