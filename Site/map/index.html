Your map

A map of your world that uncovers as you go.

Home is a live map, with your companion standing where you are. Everywhere you haven't been yet is wild land that follows the real climate — meadow and forest, desert, mountains, tundra or ice, changing with the seasons — and it clears a little at a time as you go.

Nature fog

The map is kept as patches about 35 m across. Clearing one has a short animation and the grass sways gently; with Reduce Motion on, it simply fades. Lakes and seas are drawn as water, and the major roads you've crossed show up in amber.

Where you've been

In Private, a 30-day timeline of the everyday places you stopped at — food and drink, gyms, parks, beaches and campgrounds — and the walks, runs and rides you recorded. Places like doctors' offices, lawyers or therapists are never named or kept, unless you add one yourself.

Places you return to

The places you keep coming back to, with a visit count and never dates.

Homes and a hotel

Mark up to two homes and a hotel you're staying at. The map uncovers around them, and your companion welcomes you back.

Remember this place

Anywhere you've stayed for more than a couple of minutes, you can keep the spot on your map. And with Remember places I eat out on, after three meals somewhere the blue button offers your usual there, and can find its menu by searching the web for its name, never your location.

Walk, run or ride together

Start a walk or a run from the blue button and your companion comes along. While “Save new routes” is on, the line is kept on your map, and it's logged as a walk or a run from its pace. Turn on Log my bike rides and your rides are logged as exercise by themselves; while you're riding, quick starts and banners wait until you're back on foot.

Your photos on the map

A photo you take on your own at one of those everyday places sits on the map where you took it, and you can put others there yourself. Only your own photos are ever on your map — never your friends'.

All of your map — the patches you've uncovered, your routes, places, homes and photo places — is encrypted on this iPhone only: never synced, backed up, exported, shared, given to the AI or sent to us. The one exception is your passport: its stamps, trips and flights go in the passport's own sealed backup, if you turn it on. Apple Maps draws the map and receives only the area on your screen, never your history or your walks. Fernlet uses location in the background only during a walk you started, or if you turn on background mapping, which notes the places you stop at and, while you're driving or riding, the road you take. Delete a day or a place in Private, or all of your map history in Settings → Home map.

Travel

Your companion keeps a passport.

Tap your companion on Home and its passport opens over the map. Every country, continent, ocean and sea you reach gets its own stamp, and the places you've been fill a map of the whole world.

194 country stamps

One for every country, plus the continents, the oceans and the seas. A new one shows a banner on Home, and it goes straight into the passport.

Your world

A map of everywhere you've been that zooms from the whole world down to a country, a state and a city, with your flights drawn as arcs. City cards show how much of a city you've uncovered and which of its landmarks you've found, from 144 famous ones drawn in pixels.

Airports and flights

Ten minutes at an airport earns its stamp, a layover stamps as Transit, and in the air your companion rides along with its face pressed to the window. You can also add past flights from a Flighty export file, or let Fernlet find flights in your calendar. Both are read on this iPhone and never sent anywhere.

Trips

Take a trip and your companion gets a boarding pass: say where you're going. On travel days, the planned workout becomes an easy walk and stretches and doesn't count as missed, and a hotel room gets a workout with no equipment. The trip ends by itself when you get home.
