Privacy

Privacy shouldn't depend on a promise.

Fernlet has no ads, no trackers, and no developer-run server holding your health information. The app works without an account. Your everyday information stays on your phone; if you choose iCloud sync, it goes to your own private iCloud database, not one Fernlet operates. Sensitive information — journal text, cycle data, and similar notes — is kept separately and encrypted. Your map is kept apart again: encrypted on this iPhone only, never synced, backed up, exported, shared, given to the AI or sent to us. The one exception is your companion's passport, which can go in its own sealed backup if you turn that on.

Period data, journal text and intimate notes live in a sealed, encrypted store the AI and networking code physically cannot import — a build script turns any cross-wall import into a hard compile error.
