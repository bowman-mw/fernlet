What this direction adds

  • Search that feels native. It is always visible in expanded mode and changes its prompt between recipe and workout catalogs.
  • Useful visual scanning. Full-bleed thumbnails make familiar food and movement plans recognisable before reading titles.
  • One confident handoff. Selection creates a persistent tray, so people can browse without losing the item they plan to send.
  • Fernlet’s actual geometry. The refreshed pickers use the redesign’s 20-point gutters, 20-point card corners, parchment/card tokens, and 44-point controls.
  • Privacy stays legible. The picker and received card plainly reinforce that recipe notes remain excluded from Messages.
  • Accessibility is built in. Focus rings, stateful controls, higher-contrast colors, and reduced-motion support are part of the reference—not a later polish pass.
