# Fernlet trademarks

Fernlet™ is a trademark of Michael Bowman Olay. The fern logo and the app icon are used as marks
of the official app too.

The code in this repository is licensed under Apache-2.0, and you are welcome to fork it, change it
and ship it. The license covers the code, not the name: Section 6 of Apache-2.0 grants no permission
to use the licensor's trade names or marks. This page says what that means in practice.

## What a fork may do

- Say, accurately, that it is **based on Fernlet** or **forked from Fernlet**, in its description,
  README or credits.
- Keep the copyright notices and the NOTICE file, as Apache-2.0 requires.
- Refer to Fernlet by name when comparing with it or describing compatibility.

## What a fork may not do

- Ship under the name **Fernlet**, or a name confusingly close to it, on the App Store or anywhere
  else.
- Use the fern logo or the Fernlet app icon as its own logo or icon.
- Suggest that it is the official app, or that it is made, endorsed or reviewed by the Fernlet
  developer.

## Why

The name is how people find the app that keeps Fernlet's commitments: free, with no ads, no
tracking and no developer-run server holding your data. A fork can make different choices, and that
is its right under the license. It just has to make them under its own name, so nobody installs it
expecting promises it does not make.
